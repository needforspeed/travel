* Budget US class code: W – Standard SUV
* Car-guide listing: **Chevrolet Equinox or similar**
  * 4 doors, 5 seats, Automatic, A/C, 1 Large + 2 Small bags, ~15-22 mpg
  * Features: Mixed, Smoke Free, Bluetooth SYNC, Back-up Camera, Air Bag – Driver, Power Windows, Power Locks, Power Brakes
* Dedicated rental page: **Standard SUV Rental [Ford Edge or Similar]**
  * Description: 5 seats, 1 Large + 2 Small bags. Standard-size SUV with more trunk space than Intermediate SUV.
  * Vehicle make, model, trim, features and color are not guaranteed and are subject to location and availability.

## Winter driving suitability – late December, Fairbanks AK

Fairbanks Intl Airport Budget location: 6450 Airport Way – Ste 6, Fairbanks, AK 99709, Phone 907-474-0855
Restrictions: Budget vehicles are restricted from Dempster, Denali, Dalton, Elliot, Steese, Taylor, McCarthy and unmaintained gravel/dirt roads.

Best winter-capable picks from the group:
* **Toyota 4Runner** – body-on-frame, standard 4WD with low range, high clearance. Most winter-capable.
* **Jeep Grand Cherokee** – 4WD available, good snow traction and clearance.
* **Ford Bronco** – 4WD, high clearance, designed for rough conditions.
* **Toyota Highlander AWD** – reliable 3-row AWD, good highway snow performance.
* **Honda Passport AWD** – standard AWD with good clearance.
* **Hyundai Santa Fe AWD, Kia Sorento AWD, Nissan Murano AWD** – competent AWD crossovers for plowed roads.

Use with caution:
* **Dodge Journey, Buick Encore** – lower clearance, less off-road capability; Encore is sub-compact.

General advice:
* Request AWD/4WD at booking and confirm winter tires at counter.
* Exact model and drivetrain are not guaranteed; “or similar” substitution applies.
* Check real-time availability for Fairbanks FAI for your dates; vehicle classes shown after entering pick-up dates.
