# Lassen Volcanic National Park — Labor Day Itinerary
**Sept 5–7, 2026** | SAN ⇄ SMF | Hotel: Susanville (2 nights)

---

## Logistics
- **Sat 8:00 am** — Fly SAN → SMF (~9:30). Rent car.
- **SMF → Susanville:** ~3.5–4 hrs via **CA-70** (Feather River Canyon, scenic) or CA-36 via Red Bluff/Chester.
- **Susanville → Park:** ~50 min west on **CA-44** to Manzanita Lake (NW entrance).
- **Fee:** $30/vehicle, cashless. No reservations.
- **Before you go:** check [nps.gov/lavo/conditions.htm](https://www.nps.gov/lavo/conditions.htm) — road open, Bumpass Hell status, wildfire smoke.
- **Gas up in Susanville** (park fuel unreliable). **No cell service** — download offline maps (NPS app). **Pack lunch + water.**

---

## Must-Sees (ranked)
1. **Bumpass Hell** — 3 mi RT, ~2 hrs. Boardwalk over boiling mud pots. GO EARLY (lot fills by mid-morning).
2. **Devastated Area** — 0.5 mi paved loop, 1915 eruption story.
3. **Kings Creek Falls** — 2.6 mi RT family hike to 30-ft waterfall.
4. **Kohm Yah-mah-nee VC** — exhibits, volcano film, café.
5. **Loomis Museum** (Manzanita Lake) — eruption photo exhibits + JR desk.
6. **Manzanita Lake** — easy stroll, Lassen Peak reflection.
7. **Sulphur Works** — geothermal area off the road, 10 min.
8. **Lake Helen** — photo stop beneath Lassen Peak.

*Skip Lassen Peak hike (5 mi, 2,000 ft) — admire from Lake Helen.*

## Sun Sept 6 — Full Day in Park
**Depart Susanville 7:00–7:15 am**, enter at Manzanita Lake, pay fee.
- **Morning:** Bumpass Hell (~8:15) → Sulphur Works → Lake Helen → Kohm Yah-mah-nee VC (exhibits, film, lunch).
- **Afternoon:** Kings Creek Falls hike → picnic at Summit Lake → Devastated Area → Loomis Museum.
- **Before 5 pm:** Turn in **Junior Ranger booklets**, take the pledge, get **badges** (either VC, open daily 9:30–4:30).
- Dinner back in Susanville.

## Mon Sept 7 — Last Morning + Home
- Check out ~8:30. Quick option: **Manzanita Lake loop** (1.5 mi) or **Cinder Cone @ Butte Lake** (4 mi RT, closer to Susanville).
- Leave Susanville by **12:30–1:00 pm** (CA-70) → SMF ~4:30 → evening flight home.
