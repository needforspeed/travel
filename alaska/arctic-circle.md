## Notes & caveats

- **Dalton Highway Express** (also listed on Explore Fairbanks) offers Dalton Highway ground transport but is **summer-only** and not a guided tour, so it doesn't run in your window.
- **Alaska Tours / Alaska Tour & Travel** (alaskatours.com, (907) 245-0200) is an Anchorage-based **reseller** that books Northern Alaska Tour Company's trips at higher prices (e.g. winter driving $299-339, flightseeing $649-689). Booking direct with NAT is cheaper.
- All NAT trips meet at the **East Ramp of Fairbanks International Airport** (3820 University Ave S) — you must get yourself there; some pages note a **parking fee** may apply at check-in.
- **Minimum group sizes:** NAT tours and 1st Alaska Tours require ~4 guests to run; confirm at booking. Small groups may be combined or rescheduled.
- **Meals** are not included with any of these tours (some include a sandwich/snacks or a grocery stop).
- Prices are per person, 2026-27 winter season, subject to change. Book early — the Dec 23-31 holiday window fills up.
- Driving tours are long (14-18 hrs) and run the rugged Dalton Highway; check operator policies on age limits (NAT recommends 12+ for the long drive tour).
