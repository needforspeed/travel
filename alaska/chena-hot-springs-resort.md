# Chena Hot Springs Resort – Guest Benefits & Activities

Research date: 2026-08-25  
Source: https://www.chenahotsprings.com/

## Overview

Chena Hot Springs Resort, 60 miles south of Fairbanks, operates year-round with geothermal hot springs, northern-lights viewing, and a range of guided winter/summer activities. Activities are marketed to both overnight guests and day visitors.

## Activities pricing & access

Activities are sold per person at published rates and are open to the public.

* **Dog Sledding** – $75 per person, 20 min, age 2+. Week second week Nov – first/second week Apr. Meet at Dog Yard Visitor Center. https://www.chenahotsprings.com/dog-sledding/
* **Electric Snowmobile** – From $150 per rider, ~45 min, age 16+ to operate. Zero-emission geothermal powered. https://www.chenahotsprings.com/snowmobile/
* **Aurora Viewing Tour** – $125 per person, 5 hours, age 6+. SUSV transport to Charlie Dome with heated yurts, hot drinks. No guarantee/refund for aurora. https://www.chenahotsprings.com/aurora-viewing-tour/
* **Pool House / Hot Springs** – Day pass Adults $20, Seniors $18, Children $17, 5 & under free. Open 7:00 AM – 11:45 PM daily. Outdoor hot springs lake 18+ only. https://www.chenahotsprings.com/pool-house/

Activities page lists additional options: ATV Tour $95, Dog ATV Tour $65, Side x Side ATV $75, Horseback Riding from $90, Kennel Tour Adults $25 Children $15, Ice Fishing $205, etc. https://www.chenahotsprings.com/activities-2/

## Are there guest-only benefits for activities?

No published discount or package pricing for guests was found for the commercial activities.

* Pool House FAQ:  
  *Do I need to be a hotel guest to use the pools?*  
  No. Day visitors are welcome. No overnight stay or reservation is required to enjoy the hot springs, pools, activities, or restaurant.  
  https://www.chenahotsprings.com/pool-house/
* Activity pages list the same per-person rates with no “resort guest” pricing tier.

The practical benefit of staying on site is convenience: no 1.5–2 hour drive from Fairbanks, on-site check-in at Activities Center/Dog Yard, and proximity to hot springs after activities.

## Hot springs passes with lodging

Overnight lodging bookings explicitly include hot springs access.

* Moose Lodge – “♨ Hot Springs Passes Included” https://www.chenahotsprings.com/moose-lodge/
* Bear Family Suites – “♨ Hot Springs Passes Included” https://www.chenahotsprings.com/bear-family-suites/
* Fox Rooms – “♨ Hot Springs Passes Included” https://www.chenahotsprings.com/fox-rooms/

Day visitors must purchase a Pool House day pass. Guests staying in the listed lodging types receive hot springs passes as part of the stay.

## Summary

* Activities such as dog sled, snowmobile, aurora tour, hot spring soak are available to day visitors and guests at the same published prices.
* No evidence of guest discounts for activities on the current website.
* Guest benefit = hot springs/Pool House passes included with overnight stay, plus on-site access and convenience.
