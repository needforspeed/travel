Operator found via the **Running Reindeer Ranch** website and TripAdvisor listing. Pricing, schedules and pickup details were pulled from the operator's own website.

**Note on dates:** Dec 19, 2026 - Jan 3, 2027 is the holiday **peak season** for Fairbanks tours. **Winter daylight is only ~4-5 hours** this time of year; tours run in the day and you’ll be outdoors for over an hour. Tours are reservation-only — the ranch is also a private home, so walk-ins are not accepted.

## Notes & caveats

- **Cancellation:** Full refund if cancelled at least **48 hours** before scheduled tour. No refunds after that period. See operator cancellation policy.
- **Cold weather:** Tours have run in -50°F. If it’s colder than **-20°F** and you won’t be comfortable, contact in advance for a full refund. Tours are typically only cancelled for inclement weather like icy rain.
- **Reservation only:** The ranch is also the owners’ home; no walk-ins. Book online via Peek or call 1-907-455-4998 and leave a message for same-day.
- **What to bring:** Winter boots/snow boots/Mukluks, warm mittens/hats, weather-appropriate clothing, hand warmers, cameras. Mosquito repellent not needed in winter.
- **Terrain & safety:** Uneven boreal forest trail with benches. Reindeer are tame but not trained; children must be supervised at all times, children under 3 must be in a backpack carrier.
- **Satisfaction guarantee:** 100% satisfaction guarantee offered by the ranch.
- **Other experiences at same ranch — summer only:** **Yoga with Reindeer** ($20, summer mid-May–mid-Aug, 1 hr, 1448 Ivans Alley) and **Music with Reindeer** ($20, Wednesdays 7-8pm June-Aug). Both are not available in your Dec 23-31 window.
