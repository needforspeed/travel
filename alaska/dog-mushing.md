# Dog Mushing Tours in Fairbanks (Dec 23 - Dec 31)

Companies found via the **Explore Fairbanks** (explorefairbanks.com) member/listing directory that offer dog mushing tours during your window (Dec 23-31). Pricing, schedules and pickup details were pulled from each operator's own website. **Chena Hot Springs Resort** (~60 mi NE of Fairbanks via Chena Hot Springs Rd) added per request — listed on Explore Fairbanks but outside Fairbanks proper.

**Note on dates:** Winter (snow) dog sledding runs roughly **November through April** in Fairbanks — all operators below are in season during your window unless noted. **Christmas Day (Dec 25)** and New Year's can affect availability (e.g. Rod's Alaskan Guide Service closes Wednesdays and major holidays), so confirm directly with each company. **Winter daylight is only ~4-5 hours**; most day tours run 9 AM-3 PM with multiple start times, and aurora-themed evening mushing tours are also offered.

## Notes & caveats

- **Trail Breaker Kennel** (trailbreakerkennel.com, (907) 347-7274) — the historic Susan Butcher/4-time Iditarod champion kennel, 1 mile from the airport — runs its winter **sled rides January-March only** (e.g. 4-mile Alaskan Dog Sledding $188), so it is **not in season during your Dec 23-31 window**. Its summer tours (kennel visit, Beer & Puppies) are also outside your dates.
- **Husky Homestead** (huskyhomestead.com) is a kennel tour at **Denali National Park** (Iditarod champion Jeff King), not a Fairbanks-based mushing ride — listed under Explore Fairbanks' Denali region.
- **Reseller:** Alaska Tours / Alaska Tour & Travel (alaskatours.com, (907) 245-0200) sells a "Fairbanks Winter Dog Sledding Tour" from **$244** (3 hrs incl. roundtrip hotel transfer, Dec 1 - Mar 31, min 2 people, min age 8, 250-lb limit) — it books one of the local kennels at a markup; booking direct with the kennels above is cheaper.
- **Winter clothing:** Rod's and Arctic Dog include full winter gear; Paws, Black Spruce, Sluice Box and Two Rivers provide outer layers to borrow. Come with warm base layers either way. Temps can hit negative double digits.
- **Cold-weather cancellations:** most kennels cancel or shorten tours around **-25°F to -35°F** and will refund/rebook.
- **Single riders** typically must pay for a full sled (2 seats) at Paws and Rod's; overweight guests (250-300 lb limits) must book extra seats.
- Prices are per person, 2026-27 winter season, subject to change. Book early — the Dec 23-31 holiday window fills up.
