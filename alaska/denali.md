Companies found via the **Explore Fairbanks** (explorefairbanks.com) member/listing directory that run Denali National Park trips from Fairbanks during your window (Dec 23-31). Pricing, schedules and pickup details were pulled from each operator's own website.

**Note on dates:** Dec 19, 2026 - Jan 3, 2027 is the holiday **peak season** — Northern Alaska Tour Company charges its higher rate during your window. **Christmas Day (Dec 25)** is a Friday; neither operator below runs the Denali tour on that specific day, so Christmas is not a concern for these trips. **Winter daylight is only ~4-5 hours** this time of year; both tours drive south in daylight (a bit of it) and return after dark.

## Notes & caveats

- **Alaska Railroad — Aurora Winter Train** (alaskarailroad.com, 800-544-0552) is an option for Fairbanks→Denali, but only as one-way transport, not a guided tour. In your window it runs **Saturday Dec 26 northbound** (Anchorage→Fairbanks, not useful) and **Sunday Dec 27 southbound** (Fairbanks 8:30 AM → Healy 11:45 AM → Denali 12:30 PM). There is no same-day round trip, so it only works if you stay near the park or combine with NAT's return add-on. See below.
- **NAT's Alaska Railroad add-on (+$100):** on **Saturday Dec 26** you can do the Denali Winter Drive south and take the train back to Fairbanks, arriving ~8 PM (the train flag-stops at the Denali Park Road). This add-on is non-refundable.
- **Winter park access:** the Denali Park Road is closed to vehicles beyond Mile 3 in winter. Winter visitors access the park via the **Murie Science and Learning Center / winter visitor center** near the park entrance — this is where both tours stop.
- **Resellers:** Alaska Tours / Alaska Tour & Travel (alaskatours.com, (907) 245-0200) sells NAT's Denali tour ("Winter Adventure to Denali") at the same $299-339 but with a minimum age of 6; booking direct with NAT is generally simplest. National Park Reservations (855-245-1289) resells Denali Fat Truck Tours at the same $499.
- **1st Alaska Tours** (1stalaskatours.com) and **Airlink Shuttle & Tours** (airlinkalaska.com) are Explore Fairbanks members with strong winter schedules, but neither lists a winter Denali trip from Fairbanks — their Denali offerings are summer-season.
- **Denali Backroads Adventure** (denalibackroads.com, also on Explore Fairbanks) runs summer trips to Kantishna on the park road; **no winter service**, so it does not run in your window.
- **Group minimums:** confirm at booking — NAT tours can require ~4 guests to run.
- Prices are per person, 2026-27 winter season, subject to change. Book early — the Dec 23-31 holiday window fills up.
