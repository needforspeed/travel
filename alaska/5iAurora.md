## Alerting flags

- **"Booking unavailable on Tripadvisor"** — cannot be booked through TripAdvisor/Viator; reservations are handled via **WeChat** (Ak99709) or direct contact. This is normal for smaller Fairbanks operators who book via their own website/WeChat, but it means no OTA-mediated refund/recourse — confirm terms directly.
- **One-man operation** — a single guide ("Sultan"), not a company with a fleet/backup. If he is unavailable, the tour simply doesn't run.
- **Reviews may be inflated** — high share of auto-translated reviews, some clearly from group/friend referrals, and a recent dense cluster (Jan-Mar 2026). Treat the 4.9 with caution.
- **Credentials not shown on TripAdvisor/Explore Fairbanks** — same as **every** Fairbanks operator: neither platform displays licenses/insurance/permits for anyone. Reviews also describe van-based aurora waiting and long cold Dalton Hwy drives.
