**Note on dates:** Winter snowmobiling runs roughly **November through mid-April** in Fairbanks, so all operators below are in season during your window. **Christmas Day (Dec 25)** and New Year's can affect availability (e.g. Rod's closes Wednesdays and major holidays, so Dec 23, Dec 25 & Dec 30 may be affected — confirm directly). **Winter daylight is only ~4-5 hours**; day tours run between ~10 AM-4 PM with multiple start times, and aurora-themed evening snowmobile tours (~10 PM-1 AM) are a signature Fairbanks option.

## Notes & caveats

- **Reseller (cheaper bundled option):** Alaska Active (alaskaactive.com) sells a "Guided Snow Machine Tour" in the **Chena Lakes Recreation Area** (frozen lakes, rivers, forest trails, wildlife) at **$155 (1 hr) / $270 (2 hrs)**, running **Nov 1 - Mar 31**, no experience needed, transfer **not** included (expect ~$65-70 one-way per car). It books local operators at a bundled price; booking the kennels above directly is usually cheaper.
- **Aurora snowmobile rides** are a Fairbanks specialty: Fairbanks Snow Sleighers' "Aurora Sleighers" (10 PM-1 AM, 3 hrs, **$395**) and Rod's new Aurora Snowmobile Tour (10 PM-1 AM, 3 hrs, **$395**) both run nightly in your window — the most popular (and priciest) option. Aurora sightings are never guaranteed.
- **Aurora Sleighers on Viator** (d5269-299663P2) is the same Fairbanks Snow Sleighers aurora tour, listed from **$435** — book directly at snowsleighers.com for **$395**.
- **Drive-to logistics:** Fairbanks Snow Sleighers, Rod's, and Chena are all drive-to (or +$50 pp pickup at Rod's). Winter road conditions in late December can be icy; AWD/4WD recommended. Taxis/Uber operate in Fairbanks but book return rides in advance for the ~1 AM aurora tours.
- **Gear:** all three main operators provide full winter gear (boots, bibs, jackets, gloves, helmets — heated helmets/handgrips at Rod's and Snow Sleighers). You only need warm base layers + a parka for before/after.
- **Age/weight:** drivers typically 16+ with a valid ID (Rod's: driver's license + English; Snow Sleighers: valid ID/passport). Passengers can ride double from age 5-6. Weight limits ~350-370 lb per machine. Expectant mothers and guests with neck/back problems are excluded.
- **Cold-weather cancellations:** tours can be cancelled/modified for extreme cold (approx. -25°F to -35°F) or trail conditions; operators reschedule or refund. Rod's: 90% refund/reschedule with 48+ hr notice; Snow Sleighers: 100% refund with 48+ hr notice; Chena: 24-hr notice.
- Prices are per person, 2026-27 winter season, subject to change. Book early — the Dec 23-31 holiday window fills up.
