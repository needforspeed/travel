# Winter Gear / Clothing / Boot Rental — Fairbanks (Dec 23 - Dec 31)

Where to rent cold-weather clothing (parkas/coats, snowpants/bibs, winter boots, gloves/mitts, base layers, etc.) for your Fairbanks winter trip. Compiled from Explore Fairbanks member listings + company websites, checked Aug 18, 2026.

**Key context:** Almost all Fairbanks winter **tour** operators (snowmobile, dog mushing, aurora, Arctic Circle) include full winter gear in the tour price — see `snow-mobile.md` / `dog-mushing.md`. Standalone clothing rental mainly matters if you plan **independent** aurora viewing, walking around town, or outdoor time off-tour. Both rental companies below deliver to hotels.

## Notes & caveats

- **Dec 23-31 = holiday peak season.** Both companies require reservations and both note high demand around Christmas/New Year; book as soon as trip dates are firm. Alaska Element's daily package includes gear that handles the interior's typical Dec lows (-20F to -40F).
- **Size fit matters.** Both companies do size swaps/exchanges; Alaska Element's Signature Service ($85) does an in-person fitting with extra sizes on hand. Alaska Outdoor Gear Rental sends a "traveler profile" questionnaire to match parkas/snowpants/boots.
- **Two cities, one gear flow:** Alaska Outdoor Gear Rental offers **one-way rentals** (pick up Fairbanks, return Anchorage) if your itinerary continues south.
- **Tour operators already include gear.** You may not need a separate clothing rental if every outdoor activity is a tour (they supply insulated suits/boots). A rental only pays off for off-tour aurora viewing or town walking — though for town use, layers + a warm coat often suffice.
- Neither company appears to require a deposit beyond the rental charge; both are pay-per-calendar-day (no partial-day discounts).
