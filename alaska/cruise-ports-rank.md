# Alaska Cruise Ports Rank

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hhkh_wYv8UE

## 1. Glacier Bay National Park (28:22-29:57)

* Pros: Pristine, managed wilderness; no additional cost; National Park Service ranger narration; abundant wildlife and glacier calving.
* Cons: None mentioned.


## 2. Disenchantment Bay (25:26-28:17)

* Pros: Spectacular tidewater glacier viewing (Hubbard Glacier); natural event included in cruise fare; no crowds.
* Cons: None mentioned.


## 3. Valdez and Prince William Sound (22:34-25:12)

* Pros: Dramatically beautiful fjords/mountains; Columbia Glacier; diverse marine wildlife (otters, whales, sea lions); glacier kayaking.
* Cons: None mentioned.


## 4. Haines (19:56-22:27)

* Pros: Uninterrupted natural beauty; genuine small-town feel; accessible wildlife viewing (bears/eagles) at Chilkat River.
* Cons: None mentioned.


## 5. Wrangell (17:07-19:42)

* Pros: Authentic working fishing town; Petroglyph Beach; Chief Shakes Historic Site; intimate bear viewing at Anan Wildlife Observatory.
* Cons: None mentioned.


## 6. Sitka (14:25-16:55)

* Pros: Extraordinary landscape; Alaska Raptor Center; Fortress of the Bear; Sitka National Historical Park.
* Cons: Difficult, expensive independent logistics; transport monopoly; unpredictable weather.


## 7.Downtown Ketchikan (11:36-14:15)

* Pros: Genuine fishing village character; Creek Street; local salmon/crab; Cape Fox funicular.
* Cons: Severe overcrowding from multiple ships; jewelry chain dominance; slick, crowded boardwalks.


## 8. Icy Strait Point, Hoonah (9:01-11:27)

* Pros: Humpback whale watching; historic cannery museum; proximity to authentic Hoonah village.
* Cons: Private, sterilized, corporate-controlled environment; high cost for short activities.


## 9. Skagway (6:24-8:51)

* Pros: Historical main street; Lower Dewey Lake Trail for free hiking.
* Cons: Expensive train ride; diesel engines not steam; plastic interiors; passport requirements for rail; jewelry store chains.


## 10. Juneau (3:36-6:11)

* Pros: Access to Mendenhall Glacier; Shrine of St. Therese; free hiking on Mount Roberts.
* Cons: Extreme overcrowding; Mendenhall Glacier access is difficult/bottlenecked; high cost for transfers; restricted transport options.


## 11. Ogden Point, Victoria (2:05-3:23)

* Pros: None mentioned beyond being a legal requirement.
* Cons: Short, late-night stop; limited time on land; expensive, underwhelming excursions; federal law requirement rather than a destination.


## 12. Ward Cove, Ketchikan (0:24-2:00)

* Pros: Small boat eagle watching tour.
* Cons: Industrial site 7 miles from town; expensive, limited shuttle service; long lines; high markup for food/souvenirs; lack of authenticity.
