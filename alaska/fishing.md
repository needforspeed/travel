# Winter (Ice) Fishing Tours in Fairbanks (Dec 23 - Dec 31)

Companies found via the **Explore Fairbanks** (explorefairbanks.com) member/listing directory that offer guided ice fishing during your window (Dec 23-31). Pricing, schedules and pickup details were pulled from each operator's own website. All are **in season Nov–Apr**, so all are available during your Dec 23-31 window.

**Note on dates & license:** Dec 23-31 is holiday peak — book early. **Christmas Day (Dec 25)** may affect availability (Rod's closed Wed & major holidays). **Fishing license NOT included** for any operator *except* Alaska Wilderness Enterprises — buy a **1-day Alaska sport license** at `store.adfg.alaska.gov` before you go (16+ required, under 16 free, no king stamp needed; Rod's lists $15/day but current ADF&G 1-day non-resident is **$25** per Chena site, bring signed PDF + photo ID). Guides are license vendors and can sometimes issue on site (extra fee).

## Notes & caveats

- **Explore Fairbanks source:** Fishing/River Excursions category `explorefairbanks.com/things-to-do/activities-attractions-and-tours/fishing-and-river-excursions` lists the 7 members above plus Rod's; Chena Hot Springs Resort is under Resort listing `10092` (ice fishing Nov-Apr). All are **in season Dec 23-31**.
- **License:** Alaska requires a sport license for 16+ on all waters — **Rod's, Luxury, Chena, Reel Alaskan, AK River Tours, Wild Lights, AKLARGE** = **NOT included** (buy at `store.adfg.alaska.gov` or as vendor via Rod's/Walmart/Fred Meyer, need signed PDF + photo ID). **Only Alaska Wilderness Enterprises includes** transportation + license + lunch (per `wildernessenterprises.com`).
- **Inclusions:** All include heated shelter/cabin + guide + gear/bait + drilled holes + hot beverages; many cook your catch (Rod's reindeer sausage + hot cocoa + jasmine rice; Chena + Luxury similar). Underwater cameras at Luxury (6 TVs) and some Chena Lake cabins.
- **Age/weight:** Rod's <5yo no; Luxury family-friendly; Chena 12+; Reel Alaskan family-friendly (tent/heater); Wilderness 12+? Confirm at booking.
- **Holiday:** confirm Dec 25 directly — Rod's closed Wed & major holidays, others may run but limited staffing.
- **Transport:** Rod's + Luxury + Chena + Wilderness all offer paid Fairbanks pickup/transport ($50–100pp); AK River Tours/Wild Lights often include hotel pickup — confirm at booking.
